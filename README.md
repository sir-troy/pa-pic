# Pa-pic

Photobooth software for event businesses.

**Live:** https://pa-pic.useful-indeed.com

Pa-pic turns a laptop, a camera and a photo printer into a complete photobooth for debuts, weddings, kiddie parties and company events. It comes with 30 templates, 20 themes, a drag-and-drop editor, your logo on every print, HD 600 dpi photos, auto-save and one-click printing. This website explains the software, lets you try a live demo with your own camera, and is where you message us to buy it.

## How to use

1. Try the live demo: pick a template, type a name and take 4 photos with your camera.
2. Look through the features, the 20 themes and the setup steps.
3. Send us a message with the form to buy Pa-pic for a one-time ₱999, or to ask for a walkthrough.
4. We reply by email with payment details, then send your copy and help you set it up.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. The live demo keeps your chosen template and settings in your browser's local storage so they're there next time. Camera images stay on the page and are never uploaded. If you send the contact form, your name, email, business, mobile number and message are delivered to our inbox so we can reply.
