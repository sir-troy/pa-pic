About Pa-pic

Photobooth software for event businesses.

What it does

Pa-pic turns a laptop, a camera and a photo printer into a complete photobooth for debuts, weddings, kiddie parties and company events. It comes with 30 templates, 20 themes, a drag-and-drop editor, your logo on every print, HD 600 dpi photos, auto-save and one-click printing. This website explains the software, lets you try a live demo with your own camera, and is where you message us to buy it.

How to use it

  1. Try the live demo: pick a template, type a name and take 4 photos with your camera.
  2. Look through the features, the 20 themes and the setup steps.
  3. Send us a message with the form to buy Pa-pic for a one-time ₱499, or to ask for a walkthrough.
  4. We reply by email with payment details, then send your copy and help you set it up.

Why I made this

I don't trust most “free” tools online.

Most free websites that convert a PDF or crunch your numbers do it on their own servers. Your files and data leave your device, and you have to trust that they're deleted afterwards. Very few of them can honestly promise zero data retention.

I use these every day — so I made my own.

These are tools I actually need in my day-to-day work and life. Instead of uploading my stuff to a stranger's server every time, I built small versions that run entirely in the browser. Nothing to upload, nothing to retain.

Made for me, shared with you. Why not?

I built them for personal use, but there's no reason to keep them to myself. If they're useful to me, they might be useful to you too — free, no sign-up, and your data stays with you.

Frequently asked questions

Is the demo saved or uploaded?

No. The demo uses your camera only inside the page. Photos stay on your screen and are not uploaded or saved anywhere.

How do I pay?

Send a message using the form on the Pa-pic page. We reply with the payment details and send your copy once payment is confirmed.

Is it a subscription?

No. Pa-pic is a one-time payment of ₱499. There are no monthly or per-event fees.

Your privacy, in short

Pa-pic does its work in your browser. The live demo keeps your chosen template and settings in your browser's local storage so they're there next time. Camera images stay on the page and are never uploaded. If you send the contact form, your name, email, business, mobile number and message are delivered to our inbox so we can reply.
