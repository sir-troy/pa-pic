FAQ

Questions? Sagot namin.

What equipment do I need?

A Windows or Mac laptop with Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge, a camera, and a photo printer (4R paper is the most common). That's it.

Can I use my DSLR or mirrorless camera?

Yes. Connect it by USB and use your camera brand's webcam app (Canon EOS Webcam Utility, Nikon Webcam Utility, Sony Imaging Edge Webcam, Fujifilm X Webcam), or use an HDMI capture card. A regular webcam works too.

Can it print with one click?

Yes. Pa-pic includes a ready-made shortcut that opens it in Chrome's kiosk printing mode, so pressing PRINT sends the photo straight to your default printer with no print window.

Where are the photos saved?

On your own laptop, in a folder named after your business, with a subfolder per event. Every guest gets the finished print and the original camera shots, with the date and time in the file names. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Can I use my own frame designs?

Yes. Design a frame in Canva or Photoshop, leave the photo areas see-through, and upload the PNG. Pa-pic finds the photo spots automatically. You can also build layouts in the drag & drop editor.

Does it need internet?

The booth runs on your laptop. An internet connection is recommended when you first open it so all the template fonts load.
